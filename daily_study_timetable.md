## Section 6: Anti-Burnout Protocol

| Situation | Exact Rule |
|---|---|
| Feel like quitting | Do 25 minutes only: one PYQ set or one Kannada grammar drill. After 25 minutes decide next block. |
| Cannot study full day | Emergency minimum: 2h RDPR/Karnataka GS, 1h Kannada, 1h arithmetic, 30m CA, 30m error notebook. |
| Sleep below 6h30m | Next day cap at 9h study; no late-night slot. |
| Family fight | End talk with: "I will discuss this at dinner, not now." Return to desk for 10m breathing + start easiest task. |
| Physical movement | 10 min indoor walk/stretch after breakfast, tea, dinner; 30 squats or mobility in evening. |
| Mental health checkpoint | Every Sunday write: sleep average, anger level, hopelessness level, mock trend. If hopelessness high 3 weeks, tell family/doctor. |
| Food crash | No heavy fried lunch. If sleepy after lunch, 15m eyes closed, not phone. |
| Late-night panic | Write tomorrow's first task. Stop studying at 22:30. Panic is not revision. |
